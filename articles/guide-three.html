A thoughtful buyer’s checklist

Questions to ask yourself before choosing a product or service in [YOUR TOPIC].

By [AUTHOR NAME] · Updated [MONTH YEAR]

Sample checklist: Adapt this to your subject and support any factual guidance with current sources. It does not evaluate any real product.

Before you start

  • What specific need am I trying to meet?
  • Which requirements are essential, and which are optional?
  • What is my realistic total budget, including ongoing costs?
  • Do I need professional guidance for this decision?

While researching

  • Is the information current and from a source I can identify?
  • Can I verify the specifications and terms with the provider?
  • What limitations, risks, or tradeoffs should I understand?
  • Does this recommendation include an affiliate relationship?

Before deciding

  • Have I compared alternatives using the same criteria?
  • Do I understand returns, warranties, cancellation, or support?
  • Does this choice fit my situation, rather than a generic “best” list?
